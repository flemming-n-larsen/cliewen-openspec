# Review after the walkthrough

The walkthrough attempt has ended. Keep its original observations unchanged and review the guide based on what you actually did. Do not make more project changes or fix the guide.

Write `guide-review.md` in the workspace root and answer:

1. What information, prerequisite, explanation, or command did you miss? Identify exactly where it was needed and what you did instead.
2. Which steps required assumptions or source inspection that a new developer should not need?
3. Would the instructions help a developer understand what commands change, what success looks like, and how to recover from failure?
4. Were published versus development versions, defaults, warnings, review boundaries, and archive clear?
5. Which concise text changes or diagrams would help most? Give prioritized findings with file/section references, evidence, impact, and a concrete suggested improvement. Keep preferences separate from blockers.
6. What worked well, and what could you not assess from this attempt?

If the walkthrough was blocked, say so and review only the steps actually attempted. Do not treat skipped steps or your own workarounds as successful documented behavior. End with a brief assessment of whether the guide is ready for another new developer, and why.
